ATREIA STREET · ACCOUNT

Connect your website.

Connecting enables automatic Market screen reading while the AION2 game window is visible. The companion reads listing fees, prices and settlements using on-device OCR and automatically uploads extracted transaction fields to your private account. No screenshots or unrelated screen contents are uploaded. Closing the app or signing out stops Market reading. The companion also supports separate manual synchronization of wallet and progression events.

Passwords are sent to your configured Supabase authentication endpoint and never saved to disk by this app.
