Terms of Service & Companion Data Use

Atreia Street accounts are free. Using the optional Windows companion while signed into an Atreia Street account enables automatic tracking of Aion 2 progress and Market screen observations for that account. This notice describes what the software does; it is not a claim of approval by NCSOFT.

What the companion reads

The companion passively observes Aion 2 game-network packets for character and inventory information. When you sign in to the companion it also captures the Aion 2 game window at intervals to recognize Marketplace listing, purchase, and settlement fields. It does not intentionally capture other application windows or entire monitors. Certain fullscreen modes may prevent screen reading. Game or operating-system permissions may be required.

What is sent to your account

Recognized Market observations (item names, counts, displayed prices, registration fees, settlements, observation time and linked character) are synced over HTTPS to your private account. Other companion progression and currency data may be synced as described in the app. Raw screenshots, complete recognized screen text, raw packet bytes, IP addresses, game passwords and authentication tokens are not uploaded as Market observations. A small local retry queue of structured observations may be retained when offline.

Accuracy and visibility

OCR can be wrong and a listing preview does not prove a completed trade. Screen observations are labeled as such and are not official trading receipts or financial statements. Your account-linked Market observations are intended to be accessible only to your authenticated account; database row-level security restricts other players. They are not published to public Market price feeds, rankings, or user profiles. The service cannot promise that third-party game policies permit capture.

Your choices

Using the desktop companion and signing in turns on screen-based Market observation without a second opt-in. You can stop screen-based observation by signing out of the companion or exiting the app. You may request account-data deletion. The service is unofficial, free, and provided without guarantees of accuracy or uninterrupted operation.
